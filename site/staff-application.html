Joining Predage's Staff Team means becoming part of a large group made up of Twitch Moderators, Discord Moderators, Administrators, and Management. Every staff member plays an important role in keeping the community organized, calm, and enjoyable for everyone.

By joining the team, you'll be expected to follow the usual rules of any staff team, but more importantly, the core principles of mutual respect, professionalism, collaboration, and seriousness. Keep in mind that as a staff member, you're directly representing Predage, so you're expected to behave correctly and respectfully with everyone.

It's also important to know how to work as a team, respect the decisions made by higher staff, and communicate any issues or unusual situations. Favoritism, abuse of permissions, or behavior that could harm the community or its staff won't be tolerated.

Being staff isn't just about having a role, it's about having responsibilities and actively contributing to the growth of the community.
