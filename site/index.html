Predage Info

Predage is a professional Italian Fortnite player, active in the European competitive scene. He started his competitive journey in 2020, building up experience over the years through numerous competitions and working with several organizations, including Morning Stars, Regal Esports, and Northern Star Gaming. In 2026, he joined KoS Esports, continuing his path in competitive Fortnite. Besides competing, Predage is also very active in streaming, where he shares Fortnite content and stays closely connected with his community.
