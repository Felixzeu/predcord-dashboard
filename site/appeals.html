This is where you can submit your request for a second chance on Predage's servers. If you think you made a mistake, got a punishment you'd like reviewed, or simply believe you deserve another shot at showing you can follow the community's rules, this is the right place.

Explain your situation in detail: tell us what happened, why you think you deserve a second chance, and what you plan to do differently going forward so it doesn't happen again. Your request will be reviewed by the staff team, who'll take everything you share into account along with the full context.

Be honest, respectful, and as detailed as you can — a well-explained request makes it a lot easier for staff to understand your side and make a fair call.
